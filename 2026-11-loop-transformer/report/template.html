Mechanistic interpretability

A carry through a looped Transformer

15 + 39 and 13 + 41 both make 54. Only the first sum carries a 1. If its carry transfers to the second sum, the tens column becomes 1 + 4 + 1. We swapped a hidden state and measured whether the model favored “six” more than before.

Controlled architecture comparison

Where the swap mattered

The small models started with identical outputs and saw the same training tokens within each seed. Both learned the tens digit, reaching 96.3% clean accuracy with tied weights and 95.8% with independent weights. A narrower untied model with a similar parameter count to the tied model reached 97.9%.

A swap after the first stage pushed the final score further toward the donor’s carry in the tied model. The difference was +0.320 logits, and all three training seeds agreed on its direction. By the fourth stage the average had reversed, and the seeds disagreed.

Pretrained models · arithmetic

Reading a carry in two languages

Ouro-1.4B runs 24 blocks four times. Qwen2.5-1.5B runs 28 distinct blocks, divided here into four stages. They differ in training, tokenizer and executed depth, so a difference between them cannot isolate weight sharing.

A classifier trained on stage-three hidden states could tell whether a sum needed a carry in 85–93% of held-out prompts. The models’ own first words were more uneven. Ouro gave the correct tens word in English on 98 of 100 sums and in French on 10.

Three views of a moving coin

The pilot recall checks ruled out the planned two-room follow-up.

Pretrained models · retrieval

A second copy of the clue

Each locker record placed a fruit in each of two lockers and assigned each person a locker. The model saw the record twice. We blocked the part of the second copy that describes the lockers from reading a person’s assignment in the first copy at selected depths.

{{LOCKER_SCENE}}

Across all 100 locker records

Ouro ranked the right fruit higher in 93 scenes, yet it produced that fruit as its next token in only 45. Qwen’s two scores were 55 and zero.

Cutting that same attention link at every layer reduced the correct fruit’s lead in both models. Blocking only the two selected visits to a reused Ouro block gave nearly the same interaction for the relevant and irrelevant clues. Their difference was 0.00008 logits, with a 95% interval from −0.00313 to +0.00344.
